# ava-data

Live public-source data files for the Ava passage planner, refreshed every 6 hours by a scheduled job:

- `data/tc_tracks.json` – tropical-cyclone tracks decoded from ECMWF open data (CC BY 4.0, © ECMWF)
- `data/nhc.json` – active storms from the US National Hurricane Center / Central Pacific Hurricane Center (public domain)
- `data/enso.json` – ENSO status from the NOAA Climate Prediction Center (public domain)
- `data/ports.json` – the NGA World Port Index (public domain), monthly

For awareness only. Not for navigation; always follow your official warning centre.
